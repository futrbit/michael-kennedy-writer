THE INCIDENT

Blackwood Manor, 11:47 PM

Lord Arthur Pendelton, industrialist and owner of the estate, was discovered dead in his study during a violent thunderstorm.

The door was locked. The windows were closed. Four people were inside the manor.

The attending physician initially recorded cardiac arrest. A second examination revealed traces of cyanide.
YOUR ASSIGNMENT

Find the murderer.

Investigate the manor, examine the physical evidence, question every suspect and reconstruct the final hour.

OBJECTIVE
Discover at least four pieces of evidence and determine who had the means, motive and opportunity.
INITIAL REPORT

Arthur had spent the evening entertaining four guests. At 10:30 PM he announced that someone had been stealing money from his company. At 11:15 PM the storm knocked out the manor lights. At 11:47 PM a crash was heard from the study.

The suspects claim they were elsewhere when Arthur died.
