FULL STORY & GUIDE

The Paradox Cut

The Premise
You are the last editor of a suppressed documentary about one room that has existed for 360 years. This room is a temporal nexus — the same physical space appears in 1680, 1890, 1970 and 2040. A secret bloodline (Nicholas the Alchemist → Lord Pendelton → the Vance family) has used the room to rewrite history.

Your Role
By interacting with objects in different eras you create permanent “ripples” that change the room forever and unlock new archival footage. You then arrange that footage on a multi-track timeline. The order of the clips decides which version of the truth becomes the official historical record.

How to Progress
1. Start in 1680 and spill the corrosive elixir.
2. Move to 1890 and hide the brass key.
3. Go to 1970 and broadcast frequency 104.7 FM.
4. In 2040 inspect the damaged floor to recover the vault code (7-3-9-1).
5. Add the newly unlocked clips to the timeline.
6. Pay attention to the order of the two core A-Roll clips (Bribe Offer vs Press Conference) — that order decides Arthur’s fate.
7. Render the documentary, then enter the vault code for the full conspiracy ending.

Multiple Endings
• Whistleblower Route – Arthur is the hero
• Corrupt Executive Route – Arthur is the villain
• Temporal Conspiracy Route – the room itself is exposed as the weapon
• Full Vault Ending – the entire bloodline conspiracy is revealed
