THE OWNER'S BRIEF

LIONS // TAKEOVER is a fictional business simulation created as the interactive companion to What Do Lions Eat?. It uses the public business structure of a major football club as conceptual inspiration: commercial, broadcasting and matchday revenue, alongside capital expenditure, financing, people costs and sporting performance. It is not a live Manchester United financial model, valuation, forecast or investment recommendation.
