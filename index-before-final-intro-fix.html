BOOK / 01 — ANCIENT WISDOM FOR MODERN SOULS EXCERPT / IMPERMANENCE

Life is in constant motion.

Yet as humans, we often resist change. Whether it’s clinging to a past relationship, fearing an uncertain future, or holding onto outdated beliefs, our resistance to impermanence can create unnecessary suffering. Ancient wisdom teaches us that letting go is not a loss but a powerful act of courage and freedom.

THE UNIVERSAL TRUTH OF IMPERMANENCE

From the flowing river to the changing seasons, nature constantly reminds us that nothing stays the same. Ancient traditions embrace this truth as a cornerstone of their teachings, urging us to align with the natural rhythms of life.

“This too shall pass.”— PERSIAN PROVERB
